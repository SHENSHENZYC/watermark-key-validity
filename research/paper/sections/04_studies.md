# 4 The [[paper:g_n_studies#w]] pre-registered studies: design, data and locks

Table 1 summarises the [[paper:g_n_studies#w]] studies and every pre-registered rule with its verdict. Each protocol was locked, its hash and those of every pinned code file recorded, before any outcome existed and after an independent second-model review had ended in agreement; the runner refuses to start without the lock, the analyser recomputes under it, and each report's builder re-derives every number from the record and asserts equality with the analyser's output. A failed rule stays failed. Appendix B states every rule with its tolerances; Appendix E lists the locks, the review rounds and the digests.

**Study 4 (the power side).** Qwen2.5-1.5B ([[grid:study4|model|repo]] at revision `[[tokens:study4_model|revision]]`, [[grid:study4|model|license]]) generates [[grid:study4|generation|n_new]] tokens from the first [[grid:study4|prompts|P]] tokens of [[s4:n_tx]] fresh prompts per cell under the KGW soft rule at $\gamma = 0.25$ in [[s4:n_arms#w]] arms (Table 1), for $K_4$ = [[s4:K4]] of Study 3's keys, a seeded uniform subset, on [[s4:n_cells]] English prompt cells; [[s4:N0]] unwatermarked continuations per cell are scored under every committed threshold for all [[s3:K]] evaluation keys. Thresholds come from Study 3's record; power is the share of a key's texts rejected and the cost its paired difference against the nominal threshold ([[s4:n_gen_units]] generation units; [[s4:n_contract_failures]] record-contract failures).

**Data.** The null corpora are open-content sources frozen under a hashed manifest: the Common Pile v0.1 news, Stack Exchange and Project Gutenberg subsets [@kandpal2025commonpile], with a per-document licence string, and the Wikimedia Wikipedia dumps of 2023-11-01 in [[paper:g_n_languages#w]] languages under the project licence. After licence filtering and exact and near-duplicate clustering (exact Jaccard at [[data:partition_summary|threshold_exact_jaccard]]) the partition holds [[data:n_allowed_representatives]] cluster representatives, of which [[data:partition_summary|holdout_share_of_clusters#.1%]] by cluster hash are sealed as a holdout that no study opened (`final: false` in every lock). [[s2:n_corpus#W]] confirmatory cells define the texts (Table 1): English news, Wikipedia and Stack Exchange at [[grid:study2|cells|0|L_cell]] tokens, [[paper:g_n_other_language_cells#w]] other Wikipedia languages at [[grid:study2|cells|3|L_cell]], English Wikipedia and Gutenberg at [[grid:study2|cells|8|L_cell#,]], each text length-eligible under both tokenizers. Study 4's prompts are fresh units of the [[s4:n_cells#w]] English cells, disjoint from every evaluation and calibration set.

**Keys and pairing.** Each study draws one key sample from its own namespace (validation keys and a reserved range apart, refused by code) and shares it across every cell, so comparisons across cells are paired and the key-sampling error is common to them: "$n$ of $m$ cells" counts are not independent replications. Exceptions are stated where they apply (Study 1's fixed-size draws are paired within a chain only; Study 4's power rows use [[s4:K4]] keys and its null rows all [[s3:K]]).

**Disclosures.** Two text/code discrepancies travel with their results: Study 1's R4b compared a residual with $3\,\mathrm{SE} + 10^{-9}$ where the protocol wrote $3\,\mathrm{SE}$, and Study 2's unit-variance block, listed in the protocol, is the report builder's, not the locked analyser's. Zero thresholds at a positive order index and abstaining keys (Studies 3–4) are reported as abstentions, never repaired. Each later study was designed with the earlier studies' outcomes and the key-free statistics of the realised texts known, as the protocols disclose; no per-key level of a study's own keys existed before its lock.
