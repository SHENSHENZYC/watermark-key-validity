# 8 Discussion, limitations and reproducibility

**Three readers.** A deployer who holds one key cannot read the nominal level as their own: on the frozen corpora the published repair for within-text repetition brings the key-mean near nominal and leaves one key's level dispersed around it (§5, Table D14); per-key calibration on the deployment law's null texts restores the key-mean approximately and shrinks the across-key dispersion to the reference law's scale, valid for the given key on average over its calibration draw (§6), at a power cost that is large under the raw rule at the weak strength and near zero under the distinct rule on the [[paper:g_n_models#w]] model measured (§7); a threshold set on another law, or carried to the model's own text, is a measurement with its own error, never a guarantee. A designer gets the identities: the dispersion is set by the collision structure of the weights actually scored, which the width and the retention rule control. An auditor gets vocabulary for a question now put to signatories, the measured detection error rate that the EU Code's Measure 3.2 asks them to report [@ec2026code]: for a key-randomised detector that rate is one number per key, law, tokenizer and width, not one per scheme; none of this is a compliance statement.

**Limitations.** The power side is [[paper:g_n_models#w]] model, [[s4:n_cells#w]] English prompt cells, [[paper:g_n_s4_strengths#w]] strengths and [[paper:g_n_s4_widths#w]] widths, with [[s4:K4]] keys and [[s4:n_tx]] prompts per key; a cost at δ = 0.5 is a cost at δ = 0.5. Each study shares one key sample across its cells, so its "n of m" counts are paired, not replications. Every per-key quantity is conditional on the realised texts and the frozen calibration set, with the finite-text term inside the target; no population extreme and no population dispersion of power is claimed. The predictor's structure is measured, not derived; its under-prediction at γ = 0.25 has a plausible account the studies do not isolate. The registered failures stay visible: the fixed-size statement refuted under the distinct rule, H-length refuted on real text in [[s2:s4_failing_n]] of [[s2:s4_chains]] raw-rule chains, W2 unresolved in [[s4:w2_n_rows]] of [[s4:w2_n_rows]] rows. The secondary population diagnostic of Studies 1 and 2 carries two SE constructions, neither a validated population confidence statement (Appendix D, Table D5). Nothing is a theorem.

**New versions, not re-readings.** More prompts per key or a prompt-channel interval construction for W2; a population construction with both sampling channels for the secondary diagnostic; a tie-share computation behind the raw rule's conservative calibrated level; the Gumbel-max scheme's power side, other models, samplings, attacks and schemes; stratified splits and key ensembles, named at the gate and never run. Key screening and masking, which this project never ran, were run concurrently by the course project [@yukhimchuk2026keylottery]: its measured screen keeps the 20% of keys with the smallest maximum, across the five reference domains, of the standardised reference-mean score (an audit by each key's predicted rate is proposed there, not measured), at a measured power cost of −0.4 points (95% interval [−5.1, 4.2]; 10 screened against 10 random keys with 400 prompts each at δ = 1, α = 0.0001 on clean text, OPT-1.3B), too imprecise to resolve the cost, and it selects conservative keys without removing the dispersion; masking of frequent pairs is refuted there as a remover of the dispersion, at a cost of 8.7 points at ε = 0.01 under the same δ and α in a separate experiment; neither number ranks a remedy.

**Reproducibility.** Each protocol was locked by hash before any outcome, after the reviewer's agreement; the runners refuse to start without the lock, the analysers recompute under it, and the builders re-derive every number from the record and build byte-identically. Every result and count here enters through a placeholder resolved from their registers or from the paper's own scripts, which assert [[paper:r_n_table_checks]] table and [[paper:r_n_figure_checks]] figure values against the agreed reports with [[paper:r_n_table_checks_failed]] and [[paper:r_n_figure_checks_failed]] failures; the numerals that remain in the prose are settings checked against the pinned grids or constants listed with a reason at assembly (Appendix E.2); the availability statement describes the repository and the data.
