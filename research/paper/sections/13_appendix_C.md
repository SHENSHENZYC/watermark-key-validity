# Appendix C Data

**C.1 Sources, terms and licences.** The null corpora are [[paper:g_n_sources#w]] open-content sources read from the Hugging Face Hub after their terms of use and licences were checked and quoted (`research/corpora/TERMS_OF_USE_CHECK.md`): the Common Pile v0.1 subsets `common-pile/news_filtered`, `common-pile/stackexchange_filtered` and `common-pile/project_gutenberg_filtered` [@kandpal2025commonpile], whose records carry a per-document licence string, and the Wikimedia dumps of 2023-11-01 (`wikimedia/wikipedia`, the configurations `20231101.<lang>` for [[paper:c_n_languages]] languages) under the project licence [@wikimedia2023dump]. A unit is admitted only with a licence identity on a closed allow-list ([[paper:c_licences]]; a Stack Exchange thread needs a non-empty component list with every entry admitted); [[paper:c_licence_excluded]] units were excluded by it. The freeze pinned [[paper:c_n_files]] files ([[paper:c_total_gib]] GiB) at their Hub revisions, the local SHA-256 of every data file equal to the Hub's own LFS digest (`outputs/corpora_manifest_v0.1.json`); the raw texts stay local and are not redistributed.

**C.2 Units, parsing and eligibility.** A unit is one news article ([[paper:c_news_outlets]] outlets), one Stack Exchange thread (question, answers and comments; [[paper:c_stackexchange_sites]] sites), one Project Gutenberg book, whose study window starts at token [[paper:c_book_skip]], or one Wikipedia article; [[paper:c_n_units]] units were parsed with no unparseable line, every file reconciled exactly to its card count or Parquet footer (Table C1). Token counts under both tokenizers are taken on a prefix capped at [[paper:c_cap_chars]] characters ([[paper:c_cap_chars_books]] for books) with a censoring flag; a unit is eligible at length $L$ when its Llama-3.2 count is at least $L$ (books: [[paper:c_book_skip]] + $L$), and the Qwen2.5-eligible subset is reported beside it.

**C.3 Deduplication.** Texts are normalised (NFC, lower case, whitespace collapsed, punctuation stripped); exact duplicates are found by the SHA-256 of the normalised text ([[paper:c_exact_unions]] unions) and near duplicates by MinHash ([[paper:c_minhash_perm]] permutations) of the 5-gram shingles of the normalised-text prefix with [[paper:c_lsh_bands]] × [[paper:c_lsh_rows]] LSH bands, every distinct band-sharing pair ([[paper:c_candidate_pairs]] pairs over [[paper:c_candidate_units]] units, with no estimate-based prefilter) confirmed by the exact Jaccard similarity of the full-text shingle fingerprints at or above [[paper:c_threshold]] ([[paper:c_pairs_confirmed]] pairs; [[paper:c_near_unions]] unions); CJK-dominant texts are shingled by characters; clusters are the connected components, computed across every source and language at once ([[paper:c_cross_source_clusters]] cross-source clusters); the analysis unit is one representative per cluster ([[paper:c_n_clusters]] clusters; [[paper:c_n_representatives]] allowed representatives), and the [[paper:c_duplicates]] non-representative duplicates are never scored. A planted-copy validation under the production predicate and the comparison with the pre-set rule are recorded in the audit (`research/corpora/CORPORA_AUDIT_v0.3.md`).

**C.4 The holdout and the seal.** A cluster is held out when the first sixteen hexadecimal digits of the SHA-256 of its identifier, read as an integer, are 0 modulo 5, unless it contains a unit whose text was seen before the partition ([[paper:c_exposure_hits]] exposure records; their clusters forced open): [[paper:c_n_holdout]] representatives are held out against [[paper:c_n_open]] open, [[paper:c_holdout_share_pct]]% of the clusters. The sealed accessor binds to the finalised map by its digest and the units-metadata digest, resolves every requested id against the map, opens the held-out partition only for a lock registered in an allow-list that carries `final: true`, the study name, the manifest, partition and units digests and the cells, and logs every opening; the allow-list is empty, every study's lock carries `final: false`, and no opening is logged. The [[s2:n_corpus#w]] confirmatory cells with their eligible counts are in Table C2; the proceed rule asked for at least [[paper:c_cell_floor]] eligible units per cell in each partition and held in every cell.

**C.5 The texts the studies read.** Study 2 draws $N$ = [[tokens:study2|N]] open units per cell by a seeded permutation (seed [[grid:study2|text_seed_base]]) through the seal, tokenises them under Llama-3.2-1B (`[[tokens:study2|tokenizers|llama|repo]]`, revision `[[tokens:study2|tokenizers|llama|revision]]`, $V$ = [[grid:study2|tokenizers|llama|V]]) and Qwen2.5-1.5B (`[[tokens:study2|tokenizers|qwen|repo]]`, revision `[[tokens:study2|tokenizers|qwen|revision]]`, $V$ = [[grid:study2|tokenizers|qwen|V]]), the same texts under both, and stores the token arrays under a hashed manifest. Study 3 reuses them as the evaluation sets (Gutenberg's as a [[s3:N_E_gutenberg]]-text prefix) and draws [[s3:N_C]] further calibration texts per cell as uniform permutation scans conditional on the stated exclusions (the English Wikipedia scans exclude the other English Wikipedia cell's evaluation ids), with nested prefixes at $N_{\mathrm{cal}} \in \{500, 1{,}000, 2{,}000, 5{,}000\}$. Study 4 takes fresh units of the [[s4:n_cells#w]] English cells, disjoint from every evaluation and calibration set, as prompts: the first [[grid:study4|prompts|P]] tokens of each, [[grid:study4|prompts|n_prod]] per cell, with the human continuation of [[grid:study4|prompts|L_human]] tokens kept as the quality reference; the model generates [[grid:study4|generation|n_new]] new tokens at temperature [[grid:study4|generation|temperature]] and top-$p$ [[grid:study4|generation|top_p]] under each arm's hook for the [[grid:study4|prompts|n_watermarked]] watermarked prompts per key, and the [[grid:study4|null_arm|n_texts]] unwatermarked continuations per cell form the model-null set.
