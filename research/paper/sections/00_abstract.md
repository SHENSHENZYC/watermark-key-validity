# Abstract

A text watermark detector is deployed with one secret key, but its false-positive guarantee holds on average over the key draw. We study the key-conditional false-positive rate of key-randomised detectors for a fixed null text law: its dispersion across keys, what governs it, and what restoring per-key validity costs; the phenomenon itself is the published record's. We give exact across-key identities for the score's mean shift under a text law's context-indexed tuple weights and each repeated-context retention rule, with a first-order predictor of the level's dispersion, and validate both in [[paper:g_n_studies#w]] pre-registered, locked studies: on synthetic laws and on frozen open null corpora ([[s2:n_corpus]] cells from [[paper:g_n_sources#w]] sources in [[paper:g_n_languages#w]] languages, [[s2:n_tok#w]] tokenizers, [[paper:g_n_widths#w]] context widths, [[s2:K]] keys per cell). On real text one fixed key's rate at a nominal 1% is not 1%: under the distinct rule at h = 1 the 99% key's level is [[s2:dep_distinct_h1_q99_range]] across the [[paper:g_n_units#w]] (cell, tokenizer) units, and the predictor passes as registered with a measured under-prediction of [[paper:g_s1_underprediction_words]] in the primary block. Per-key conformal calibration on [[grid:study3|N_cal_set#,]] null texts of the deployment law reduces the across-key SD from [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_nominal_sd_median]] to [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal5000_sd_median]] pp (distinct rule, h = 1, α = 0.01; medians over the units), and a threshold transferred between an English cell and another language's disperses about [[s3:transfer_factor_english_other]] times more across keys than on its own law (medians over such pairs at one setting). On [[paper:g_n_models#w]] model's watermarked generations (Qwen2.5-1.5B; δ = 0.5, h = 1, L = 256, α = 0.01; [[s4:K4]] keys with [[s4:n_tx]] prompts each), per-key conformal calibration on [[grid:study3|N_cal_set#,]] same-cell human null texts costs a median [[paper:t6_cost_conformal5000_raw_median_range]] pp of power across [[s4:n_cells#w]] prompt cells under the raw rule and [[paper:t6_cost_conformal5000_distinct_median_range]] pp under the distinct rule; the across-key dispersion of power after calibration is unresolved. Nothing here is a theorem, a guarantee or a ranking; every per-key statement is conditional on the realised texts and the frozen calibration set.
