## 7.5 Operating points, transfer sources and the mechanism

Over the [[s4:K4]] power keys under the nominal threshold, [[s4:op_news_nominal_share_above_alpha_pct]] / [[s4:op_wikipedia_nominal_share_above_alpha_pct]] / [[s4:op_stackexchange_nominal_share_above_alpha_pct]]% have a stored corpus level above α and [[s4:op_news_nominal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]] / [[s4:op_wikipedia_nominal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]] / [[s4:op_stackexchange_nominal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]]% a model-null level above α; conformal calibration at N_cal = 5,000 brings the shares to [[s4:op_news_conformal_share_above_alpha_pct]] / [[s4:op_wikipedia_conformal_share_above_alpha_pct]] / [[s4:op_stackexchange_conformal_share_above_alpha_pct]]% and [[s4:op_news_conformal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]] / [[s4:op_wikipedia_conformal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]] / [[s4:op_stackexchange_conformal_share_above_alpha_modelnull_pct]]%, so in Stack Exchange a calibration on human null text does not carry key by key to the model's own text. Calibration weakens the association between a key's null level and its power (the correlations under every method are in Table D7b). A threshold transferred from another cell buys power with level (the news source applied to Stack Exchange: [[s4:transfer_stackexchange_from_news_raw_power_pp]] pp of power with [[s4:transfer_stackexchange_from_news_raw_share_pct]]% of the evaluation keys above α on the model's null text; Appendix D8); no transfer guarantee follows. A key's stored null level correlates with its nominal power at [[s4:mech_news_corr_null_power]] / [[s4:mech_wikipedia_corr_null_power]] / [[s4:mech_stackexchange_corr_null_power]] and with its green share under the hook at [[s4:mech_news_corr_null_green]] / [[s4:mech_wikipedia_corr_null_green]] / [[s4:mech_stackexchange_corr_null_green]], consistent with the hypothesis that a key whose hash colours the model's likely tokens green gets both a higher null level and a stronger watermark: a descriptive association, not a variance decomposition. No text was excluded for quality (loop shares in Table D9a).
