**B.1 The adequacy rules (R1, S1, T1, W3).** The statistic is the ratio of the observed across-key SD to the predicted one with its key-bootstrap 95% interval ([[grid:study3|key_bootstrap_B]] resamples of the keys); a cell is consistent when the interval lies inside [2/3, 3/2], inconsistent when it is disjoint from the band and unresolved otherwise; the rule passes with at least [[grid:study2|primary|min_resolved]] resolved cells and at least [[grid:study2|primary|pass_share#.0%]] of the resolved consistent (W3: [[grid:study4|W3|min_resolved]] and [[grid:study4|W3|min_consistent_share#.0%]]). Eligibility: a theory share of at least [[grid:study2|primary|min_theory_share]], with a mean $M$ of at least [[grid:study2|primary|min_mean_M]] in Studies 1 and 2 and, in Studies 3 and 4, no flag on the prediction and (W3) a positive predicted SD. The candidate, eligible, resolved, consistent, inconsistent and unresolved counts are always reported together (Tables B1–B4); every non-consistent cell or row is listed with its diagnostics in Tables D4a–D4d.

**B.2 The audits (R2, S2) and the identities (R3, S3).** R2 and S2 compare the key-mean level $\bar a$ with its exact value $\mu_a$ given the texts (the mean over texts of the binomial tail under the distinct rule; exactly $\alpha$ for Gumbel-max) by the two-sided Hoeffding–KL bound $\min\{1, 2\exp(-K\,\mathrm{KL}(\bar a \,\|\, \mu_a))\}$, flagged below $10^{-6}$; the per-check false-audit probability is at most $10^{-6}$ for any $K$ and, the keys being independent draws of the ideal key law given the texts, under any dependence between texts and between checks or cells (not under dependence between the key draws themselves), and the experiment-level one is at most the number of checks times $10^{-6}$ by the union bound ([[s1:r2_checks]] and [[s2:s2_checks]] checks, none flagged); the bound cannot flag under-rejection at $\alpha = 0.01$ in most cells and flags a dead detector only where $2(1 - \mu_a)^K < 10^{-6}$, with the flaggable thresholds tabulated in each study's validation record. R3 and S3 are the identity checks of Appendix A.1 with the gross-failure threshold $6\,\mathrm{SE} + \tau$, $\tau = 10^{-9} g(1-g) A$ for the green fraction and $10^{-9} A$ for the Gumbel-max mean score; structural zeros (the fixed-size variance vanishing when $V A - B \le 10^{-9} V A$) require $s_0^2 \le \tau$; the nominal-95% coverage of the key-bootstrap intervals is a diagnostic. The 6-SE threshold is heuristic: $s_0^2$ is a mean of $K$ i.i.d. terms (bounded for the green fraction; with finite fourth moments, not bounded, for the Exp(1) scores), central at the studies' $K$ by the validation's disjoint subsamples, and no experiment-level probability is claimed for it.

**B.3 Length and width (R4, R4b, R5, S4, S5).** The tolerances are Appendix A.3's. On real text S4 fails in [[s2:s4_failing_n]] of [[s2:s4_chains]] chains, listed in Table B2: [[s2:s4_fail_h4]] at $h = 4$ ([[s2:s4_fail_h4_nonnews_n]] of them outside the news register, where the predictor's decomposition puts the finite-text term first), [[s2:s4_fail_news]] news chains at $h = 1$–$4$, where the between-text collision itself falls with length, and [[s2:s4_fail_h1_nonnews_n]] further $h = 1$ chains; the [[paper:g_s2_s4_groups#w]] groups overlap, a news chain at $h = 4$ belonging to both the $h = 4$ group and the news group. The lattice of the $z$-test's exact size at $\gamma = 0.25$ under the raw rule, which a reported "false-positive rate at 1%" inherits, is [[s2:exact_size_h1_L64]], [[s2:exact_size_h1_L128]] and [[s2:exact_size_h1_L256]] at $h = 1$ for $L \in \{64, 128, 256\}$ ($M = L - 1$), and [[s2:exact_size_h4_L64]], [[s2:exact_size_h4_L128]] and [[s2:exact_size_h4_L256]] at $h = 4$ ($M$ = [[s2:M_h4_L64]], [[s2:M_h4_L128]], [[s2:M_h4_L256]]); a plausible account of the $h = 4$ dip, not an identified decomposition.

**B.4 Study 3's rules (T1, T2, REP).** T2 takes the material pairs ($R_{\mathrm{pred}}$ outside [2/3, 3/2]) and counts a pair as agreeing when the paired key-bootstrap interval of $R_{\mathrm{obs}}$ excludes 1 on the predicted side, disagreeing when it excludes 1 on the other side and unresolved otherwise; it passes with at least [[grid:study3|primary|min_resolved]] resolved and at least [[grid:study3|primary|T2_pass_share#.0%]] agreeing. REP compares Study 3's nominal across-key SD with Study 2's on the same texts under independent keys for every (tokenizer, evaluation cell except Gutenberg, width, rule) of the KGW family at $\alpha = 0.01$, a gross failure at $|\mathrm{SD}_3 - \mathrm{SD}_2| > 6\sqrt{\mathrm{se}_3^2 + \mathrm{se}_2^2} + 10^{-12}$ ([[s3:rep_checks]] checks, [[s3:rep_gross]] gross failures). The diagonal at $N_{\mathrm{cal}}$ = [[grid:study3|N_cal_set#,]] is never T1-eligible (the Beta term's SD is of the order of the finite-evaluation floor, so the theory share lies below the floor) and is reported descriptively against the reference law.

**B.5 Study 4's rules (W1, W2, W3), the record contract and the budget stop.** W1's tolerance is $c = \max(t,\, 2\,\mathrm{SE})$ with $t$ = [[grid:study4|W1|tolerance_pp]] pp and SE the cluster-bootstrap SE of the predicted key-median, recorded before the lock; it passes with at least [[grid:study4|W1|min_consistent_cells]] of [[s4:n_cells]] cells consistent and at most [[grid:study4|W1|max_inconsistent_cells]] inconsistent. W2 passes with at least [[grid:study4|W2|min_agree_rows]] of [[s4:w2_n_rows]] rows agreeing and at most [[grid:study4|W2|max_disagree_rows]] disagreeing, the raw rule's pre-stated side "below 1" and the distinct rule's "inside [2/3, 3/2]". W3 is B.1's rule on the model-null block. Every generation unit's stored green flags must equal the locked detector's recomputation at every scored position; a unit that fails is a contract failure and enters no rule ([[s4:n_contract_failures]] of [[s4:n_gen_units]] units failed; a run with more than [[grid:study4|integrity|max_contract_failure_share#.0%]] failures would have been void). A committed threshold at or below 0 never rejects (an abstention, never repaired): [[s3:zc_zero_positive_j_n]] stored thresholds of Study 3 are zero at a positive order index (a stored $p$-value of exactly 0, its numerical cause not isolated), beside the structural abstentions of the tolerance and conformal methods at $\alpha = 0.001$ and small $N_{\mathrm{cal}}$; Table D10d lists the [[s4:n_null_abst_settings]] null-block settings with abstaining keys. A text with rep-4 above [[grid:study4|integrity|loop_bar_rep4]] is a loop, reported and never excluded. The runner's budget stop ([[grid:study4|stop|max_hours_factor_over_projection#g]] times the validator's projection per invocation) was not reached.

**B.6 Disclosed text/code discrepancies.** (i) Study 1's R4b: the locked analyser compared the residual with $3\,\mathrm{SE} + 10^{-9}$ where the protocol's text wrote $3\,\mathrm{SE}$; the residuals were floating-point remainders of an exactly saturated quantity and the SEs smaller still, so the literal text fails in every cell and the implemented rule passes; the verdict stays the locked rule's ("reproduced") and the discrepancy travels with it. (ii) Study 2's unit-$\sigma$ secondary block is listed in the protocol and computed by the report builder, not by the locked analyser; it is descriptive. Every later protocol states every tolerance its code applies.

Study 2 draws [[grid:study2|K]] keys from seed [[lock:study2|keys|production_start]], shared across every cell, width, length, rule, family and tokenizer.
