# Code and data availability

The companion repository (`https://github.com/SHENSHENZYC/watermark-key-validity`, a public snapshot of the private working repository, with the paper's page at `https://shenshenzyc.github.io/watermark-key-validity/`) holds the [[paper:g_n_studies#w]] protocols, their pre-run locks with the hashes of every pinned file, the run records (per-cell outputs, run manifests and the locked analysers' results), the report builders with their generated reports, the paper's table, figure, fill and assembly scripts, the review prompts, memos and resolutions, and the hashed manifests of every external input. The null corpora are open-content sources frozen under those manifests from the Hugging Face Hub at pinned revisions and verified by SHA-256 against the Hub's own digests: the Common Pile v0.1 subsets `common-pile/news_filtered`, `common-pile/stackexchange_filtered` and `common-pile/project_gutenberg_filtered` [@kandpal2025commonpile], whose per-document licence string is recorded per unit, and the Wikimedia dumps of 2023-11-01 in [[paper:g_n_languages#w]] languages (`wikimedia/wikipedia`, under the project licence) [@wikimedia2023dump]; the raw texts are not redistributed and are re-derivable from the manifests. The tokenizers are Llama-3.2-1B and Qwen2.5-1.5B at the pinned revisions, and Study 4's generations come from Qwen2.5-1.5B ([[grid:study4|model|license]]) at revision `[[tokens:study4_model|revision]]` under the locked runner. The held-out partition ([[data:partition_summary|holdout_share_of_clusters#.1%]] of the duplicate clusters by hash) was never opened: every lock carries `final: false`, and the access layer refuses the holdout without a registered final lock and logs any opening. The per-text records of every study are committed in full; the one file above the repository host's size limit, Study 3's locked-analyser output (`results.json`), is committed gzipped with both digests in its run manifest and is regenerable from the committed record by the locked analyser.
