# AI use and independent review

Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), run in Claude Code, drafted the prose of this paper and wrote the code of the [[paper:g_n_studies#w]] studies, their analysers, report builders, table and figure scripts and the assembly of this document, under the author's instruction and section-level review; the author took every design, lock, wording and publication decision, read every word, and takes full responsibility for the content. An independent second model, Codex (OpenAI), reviewed every major checkpoint before the project proceeded: [[paper:r_n_checkpoints_agreed]] checkpoints ([[paper:r_checkpoint_range]]: the topic lock, the data verdict, each study's protocol before its lock, each study's report, the claim gate, the assembled draft of this paper and the paper as typeset before its publication) over [[paper:r_n_rounds]] rounds with [[paper:r_n_findings]] numbered findings, each verified by the drafting model against the primary evidence before any change and answered in a response memo, every judgement call put to the author, and no checkpoint passed without the reviewer's agreement and no open dispute (Appendix E, Table E1). [[paper:r_open_sentence]] Neither model is an author. The literature screens were run by the drafting model and its delegated agents against the arXiv API, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, GitHub and the web, and every decisive quotation was then confirmed by substring search in the cited full text; the bibliography was built from primary sources and validated by a citation checker; the watermarked and unwatermarked texts of Study 4 are outputs of the open-weight model under study, generated by the locked runner, not by an assistant.
