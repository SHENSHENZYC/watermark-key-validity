## 6.1 Per-key calibration on the deployment law (Table 4, Figure 4)

With N_cal null texts of the deployment law, per-key conformal calibration brings the key-mean level near nominal under the distinct rule ([[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal5000_keymean_range]] across the [[paper:g_n_units#w]] units at N_cal = 5,000) and below it under the raw rule ([[s3:rem_h1_raw_conformal5000_keymean_range]]; a lattice-tie account, suggestive), and reduces the across-key dispersion from the nominal [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_nominal_sd_median]] pp to [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal1000_sd_median]] pp at N_cal = 1,000 and [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal5000_sd_median]] pp at N_cal = 5,000 (distinct rule, h = 1; medians over the units), the level the i.i.d. reference law gives for the calibration order statistic plus the finite-text floor. Under the reference law about half the keys sit above α, the order statistic's level being centred near α: the measured share is [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal5000_share_alpha_median]] in the median unit against a finite-evaluation prediction of [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_conformal5000_pred_share_finite_median]], the reference law's population share being [[s3:diag_conformal5000_h1_distinct_beta_share]], a model quantity. The tolerance method at δ = 5% and N_cal = 5,000 puts [[paper:t4_distinct_tolerance5000_pred_share_pop]]% of keys above α in population under the reference law, while on [[s3:N_E]] evaluation texts the measured share is [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_tolerance5000_share_alpha_median]] in the median unit against a finite-evaluation prediction of [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_tolerance5000_pred_share_finite_median]]. The calibrated-threshold predictor passes as registered (T1: [[s3:t1_consistent]] of [[s3:t1_resolved]] resolved rows consistent; eligible ratios median [[s3:t1_ratio_median]]), centred where Study 2's fixed-threshold predictor was not, every non-consistent row under the raw rule on [[s3:t1_fail_n_tagE#w]] evaluation cells. The reference law is a model whose adequacy was measured, not a theorem, and validity for the given key means validity on average over that key's calibration draw (conformal) or with the stated failure probability over draws (tolerance), never for every frozen calibration set; per-key calibration as practice is the record's [@vovk2012conditional; @xie2025classroom; @aremu2026activation; @genhatharan2026nominal; @delbianco2026compliance], and the concurrent project standardises each key's score on same-domain reference text over 1,000 keys, after which its key-averaged false-positive rate and its upper keys remain above the nominal and ideal levels [@yukhimchuk2026keylottery].

## 6.2 AutoMark's multiplier and the plug-in comparators

Proposition 1 multiplies a p-value sound over the key by 1/β. Under the distinct rule, where that prerequisite holds asymptotically, the multiplier is conservative on these texts (key-mean [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_multiplier20_keymean_median]], no key above α in any of the [[paper:g_n_units]] units at h = 1); under the raw rule, which violates the prerequisite, [[s3:rem_h1_raw_multiplier20_share_alpha_median]] of the keys exceed α in the median unit, a property of the raw rule and not of their proposition [@gloaguen2026automark]. The smallest empirical multiplier fitted on disjoint keys varies by rule (κ̂ [[s3:zc_h1_distinct_empirical_kappa_median]] under the distinct rule against [[s3:zc_h1_raw_empirical_kappa_median]] under the raw) and by cell; its measured share of keys above α is at or below β in most units (above β = 5% in [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_empirical_units_share_above_beta]] of [[paper:g_n_units]] distinct-rule and [[s3:rem_h1_raw_empirical_units_share_above_beta]] of [[paper:g_n_units]] raw-rule units). The pooled calibration targets the key-average without per-key control: under the distinct rule it restores the key-mean ([[s3:rem_h1_distinct_pooled_keymean_median]]) with an across-key SD of [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_pooled_sd_median]] pp against the nominal [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_nominal_sd_median]] pp and [[s3:rem_h1_distinct_pooled_share_alpha_median]] of the keys above α (Table 4 gives the raw rule, where lowering the inflated mean lowers the absolute SD); pooled null recalibration is practised [@lichen2026chainmark; @nemecek2026crosslingual].
