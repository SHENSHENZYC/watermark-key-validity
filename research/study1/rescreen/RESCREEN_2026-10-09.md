## Correction (2026-10-09, evening; R03 round 1, F1)
The reading above was wrong. The R03 reviewer read AutoMark (arXiv 2609.37310, retrieved by query 6 and dismissed here from its title) at the section level and found that it **defines "soundness given a key"** as a PAC-over-keys requirement (§3.2, Eq. 3), **tests it** (Algorithm 3) and **proves a multiplier remedy** (Proposition 1, App. I), with its cost evaluated (App. C). The reviewer's broader web search also found a GitHub-published paper not on arXiv, **Genhatharan 2026, "When Nominal False-Positive Rates Fail"** (`nisaharan/when-nominal-false-positive-rates-fail`, release v1, 2026-09-11), which measures KGW's per-key false-positive rate over ten keys and a 128-key sweep, names the mean-shift mechanism and its √T growth, shows the tokenizer dependence and prices per-key, per-length calibration. Both are now in the gate (`research/KEYCOND_GATE_v0.3.md`) with a claim-by-claim comparison. Lessons: a retrieved paper by a group already on the scoop list is read at the section level before it is filed, and every pre-lock screen includes a GitHub search for papers published only in repositories.
