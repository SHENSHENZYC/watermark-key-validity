## Q1 Null and what is random
Observed. Detection (p.5) reduces "to testing for independence between the tokens v and the seeds s reconstructed via the" / "detector’s key. We define the null and alternative hypotheses as follows (Huang et al., 2023):" — "• Null hypothesis H0 : The text v is generated independently of the seeds s (e.g., by a" / "human or an unwatermarked model)." Type I error (p.6): "The Type I error (false positive rate) measures the probability of incorrectly identifying" / "non-watermarked text (e.g., human-written) as watermarked. Given the high stakes of false" / "accusations (e.g., in academic integrity), this error must be bounded by a small significance" level: "PH0 (D(V, S) = Watermarked) ≤ α." Model-agnosticity (Definition 2.2, p.6): "PW is chosen independently of the target distribution q. That is, the distribution of seeds S" / "does not depend on the model’s logits." Seed law (p.7): "Leveraging its role as a known reference, we designate p0 as the marginal" / "distribution for the watermark signal (or random seed), denoted as s. Therefore, the seed" space equals the vocabulary. Formal hypotheses (p.8): "H0 : v and s are sampled independently," / "H1 : (v, s) are sampled from the joint coupling w." Validity constraint (p.8, eq. 1): the e-value "must be defined prior to observing the specific target distribution q. The design of the" e-value uses only q ∈ Q(p0, δ); "neighborhood, i.e., q ∈ Q(p0 , δ). Consequently, to ensure validity, the e-value must satisfy" / "the expectation bound uniformly across the entire uncertainty set Q:" / "sup Ev∼q,s∼p0 [e(v, s)] ≤ 1." Sequential guarantee (Theorem 2.4, Ville, p.7): "This result guarantees that a researcher can track the e-value process continuously and" / "stop at any data-dependent time τ (a stopping time) while maintaining Type-I error control." and "Specifically, EP [Eτ ] ≤ 1 holds for any stopping time τ (possibly unbounded), a kind of" guarantee not available for p-values.

Reader's inference. Under H0 both the text v ∼ q (q unknown within an ℓ1-ball of the anchor) and the seed s ∼ p0 are random, and the e-value constraint is an expectation over both. The seed is the key-derived object (the seeds are "reconstructed via the" / "detector’s key."), so the Type I bound is marginal over seeds/keys; nothing conditions on a fixed realised key, and the paper does not say how a deterministic key-plus-context seed generator yields s exactly distributed as p0.

## Q3 Empirical FPR procedure
Observed. No empirical false-positive rate on null text is reported. Level (p.15): "All experiments enforce a Type-I error constraint of α = 0.02. Lower values indicate higher" efficiency. Setup (p.14): "Setup. We select Llama2-7B-chat (Touvron et al., 2023) with temperature 0.7 as target" / "distribution (i.e., the model to be watermarked) and Phi-3-mini-128k-instruct (Abdin et al.," 2024) as the anchor; the MarkMyWords benchmark "generates 300 outputs spanning three tasks—book summarization," creative writing and news, then perturbs them; "Detection is run in a sequential test setting with early stopping. For baseline methods, we apply Bonferroni corrections to preserve" / "anytime valid Type-I error guarantees: let pk denote the p-value at time step k ∈ Z+ , reject" at the first k with p_k < α/(k(k+1)). Metric (p.14): "Size represents the median number of tokens required to" / "detect the watermark at a given p-value, computed over a range of perturbations listed above." Synthetic check (p.13): "For each α, we compute 10000" / "stopping-times by generating sequences (V t , S t )t≥1 ∼ w∗ and computing the resulting" log e*, for α from 1e-2 to 1e-120 (Fig. 2, p.14). Appendix D (p.51): temperatures 0.3, 0.7, 1.0; "Table 2 reports generation quality across watermarking schemes." and Table 3 reports size.

Reader's inference. Only power-side quantities (tokens-to-detect at nominal α) are measured; there is no null corpus, no empirical FPR, no comparison with the nominal level, and no per-key FPR. The "median over different private keys" is a median of detection size across keys, which incidentally shows the authors re-ran with several keys but did not study the null side across them.

## Reader's inference
The validity statement is an expectation over the seed s ∼ p0 and the text v ∼ q; the key is only the device reconstructing s. The paper's single multi-key remark (median detection size over private keys) shows awareness that results vary by key on the power side, but the false-positive side is never measured, for one key or several.
