## Reader's inference
NIST frames watermark-detector reliability in TPR@FPR vocabulary, insists that false positives are the costly error, notes that detection of keyed watermarks requires the key and is therefore private, and warns that averaged metrics can hide important edge cases. It supplies the policy vocabulary and motivation for asking what the false-positive rate is under one deployed key, but it does not itself raise key-conditional or configuration-conditional error rates.
