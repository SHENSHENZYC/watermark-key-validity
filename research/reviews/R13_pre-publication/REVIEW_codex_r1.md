The variance correspondence in final-gate §2.3 checks algebraically. Write the other project's weight as

\[
a_u=\frac1n\sum_x\frac{A_{xu}}{\sqrt{T_x}},\qquad S_c=\sum_{u:\,\operatorname{context}(u)=c}a_u.
\]

Its independent-colouring variance is \(\sum_u a_u^2\), and its fixed-size variance is \((V\sum_u a_u^2-\sum_c S_c^2)/(V-1)\). Here its \(a_u\) is this paper's \(b(u)\); this paper's \(w_z(u)\) divides it by \(\sqrt{\gamma(1-\gamma)}\). Substitution into this paper's independent and fixed-size formulae gives exactly the same pair. The other implementation allows multiplicities for `all` and indicators for `uniq`/masking; the overlap is not confined to the deduplicated rule. This is a genuine overlap in the identities, as the gate now acknowledges. [Source: Proposition 1 and the weight construction](https://github.com/katcinskiy/keylottery/blob/8ebb6b4f52927d0adbe9084d53ba49f3f825d6eb/proposal/proposal.tex), [implementation](https://github.com/katcinskiy/keylottery/blob/8ebb6b4f52927d0adbe9084d53ba49f3f825d6eb/kl/nullstats.py).

**Evidence.** `research/paper/sections/06_remedies.md:5`, assembled §6.1 (`PAPER_DRAFT_v0.1.md:141`), says the concurrent standardisation “fixes its key-mean.” This paragraph's estimand is the key-mean false-positive level. [C7 source](https://github.com/katcinskiy/keylottery/blob/8ebb6b4f52927d0adbe9084d53ba49f3f825d6eb/report/claims.json).

**Recommendation.** Say that the concurrent work standardises each key's score using same-domain reference text, while its measured key-mean FPR and upper-key tail remain above the nominal/ideal benchmark.
