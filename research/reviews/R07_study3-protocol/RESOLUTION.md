- For the report: the finite-evaluation share is the estimand comparable with a measured share; the population share is a model quantity; both are reported with their labels. The diagonal at N_cal = 5,000 is descriptive, with the floor stated, and T1's pass says nothing about adequacy there. The multiplier arm is interpreted under Proposition 1's prerequisite (distinct rules; the raw rule violates it), and the empirical plug-in is never presented as AutoMark's test. The exceedance diagnostic is a reference, not a PAC-over-keys check. Every "same law" and "near-identical" label is a design approximation. A finite sample of convergence settings is evidence on those settings, not a uniform error bound; absolute, not relative, accuracy is claimed for rare tails.
- For Study 4: pre-specify how keys and settings are selected from the committed thresholds record, retain the exact threshold-to-key mapping, and report abstentions and any null-law mismatch (the reviewer's round-1 recommendation).
- For the paper and the claim gate: Nemecek et al. 2026 compare a pooled cross-language threshold with per-language thresholds and do not characterise variation across keys; per-language calibration, the failure of nominal thresholds across languages and the pooled comparison are not new. Confirm the HTML-based reading against the PDF at the claim gate.
