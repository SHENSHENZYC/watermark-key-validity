## Implementation addendum (2026-10-10, after D5; for round 2)
Everything above marked "to implement" is implemented in `research/STUDY1_PROTOCOL_v0.2.md`, `research/study1/s1_core.py`, `s1_run.py`, `s1_analyze.py`, `s1_validate.py`, `grid_v0.2.json` and `tools/key_policy.py`; the validation record is `outputs/study1_v0.2/VALIDATION.json`. Four points go beyond, or refine, what the dispositions said, and are flagged here so the reviewer need not hunt for them:
1. **F4, the predictor.** The round-1 disposition named φ(z_α)·√(exact identity at ŵ_z). Implementing it showed that this delta-method form fails under the first-order model itself once the z-shift SD exceeds about 0.3 (at α = 0.01: factor 1.22 at 0.25, 1.89 at 0.5, 3.7 at 1.0; the h = 0 cells reach 1–5). The predictor is therefore the normal **mixture** of the gate's level approximation, α_k = Φ̄((z_α − δ_k)/σ) with δ_k ~ N(μ_δ, ρ̄), plus the finite-text term E[α_k(1−α_k)]/N_eff that the conditional target carries (texts independent given the key); μ_δ, ρ̄ and σ² come from the identities at the realised weights (protocol v0.2 §3). ρ̄ is the average pairwise across-key covariance of two texts' scores, which removes the finite-text floor the reviewer computed (0.042 pp) instead of carrying it. The linearised form and the unit-σ variant are reported as diagnostics. R1's eligibility adds a theory share ≥ 0.75 so that the finite-text term alone cannot pass the band.
2. **F6, R2's calibration.** Subsampling 500 keys from 20,000 on a high-dispersion validation cell gives chance failure rates of the 3-SE check of 0.0%, 0.7% and 3.3% at α = 0.05, 0.01, 0.001 (skewness of the per-key level up to about 10). R2 is therefore decided at α ∈ {0.05, 0.01} with "≥ 97% within 3 SE and none beyond 5 SE"; α = 0.001 is reported. *[Correction, 2026-10-10, round 2 (F16): those three rates came from a scratch experiment before the validator ran; the recorded values in the v0.2 `VALIDATION.json` differ (0.75%, 0.75%, 1.5% at γ = 0.25 and 1.0%, 0.5%, 5.5% at γ = 0.5, each from 400 subsamples), and the rule itself is replaced in protocol v0.3 by Hoeffding's KL bound.]*
3. **Shared texts across widths.** The chain for each h of a law now re-derives the tuples from the same texts (one seed per law), so R5's width comparison is paired; the text-bootstrap tolerance is then conservative.
4. **Output root.** Study 1's outputs move to `outputs/study1_v0.2/`; the v0.1 pilot (210 cells, production keys 0–499) stays under `outputs/study1_v0.1/validation/` as a retained pilot and is the reason production keys moved to 20,000+.
Smoke tests on a tiny grid exercised every path (degenerate markers, resume with matching provenance, the inventory refusal); the small-K artefacts they showed (R2 and R3 "failures" at K = 64) were traced to key-sampling noise and skew, not to the simulator (20,000-key checks in the validation agree with the exact binomial at every α and γ). *[Correction, 2026-10-10, round 2 (F16): the recorded largest deviation is 1.16 SE, not 0.3; the earlier figure came from the scratch experiment.]*
