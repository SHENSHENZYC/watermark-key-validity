# Independent reviews (rule A7 of the research-project-guide skill)

One folder per major checkpoint, numbered in the order reached: `R<NN>_<slug>/` holding `PROMPT.md` (written by the
conductor), `REVIEW_<reviewer>_r<k>.md` (written by the reviewer, round k), `RESPONSE_<conductor>_r<k>.md` (the
conductor's verification and actions) and, once the verdict is AGREE with no open dispute, `RESOLUTION.md`.
`REVIEW_ORIENTATION.md` is the reviewer's self-contained briefing and is kept current by the conductor.
