# C06 — Shift-robust conformal selective answering for LLMs (search-level screen, 2026-10-08)

## Candidate (one sentence)
Under topic or difficulty shift between calibration and deployment prompts, how much coverage do conformal abstention rules for LLM answers lose, does weighted conformal prediction with embedding-based density ratios restore it, and can a cheap exchangeability diagnostic predict the loss (comparators: split conformal for LLMs; weighted conformal, Tibshirani et al. 2019; conformal beyond exchangeability, Barber et al. 2023)?

## Occupied (what the candidate can no longer claim)
- "Split conformal for LLMs loses coverage under topic/domain shift" is already shown (Kumar et al. 2023 for out-of-subject MCQ; DS-CP on MMLU; HG-CRC's 47% budget violations; the RAG factuality robustness paper 2603.16817; configuration shift 2608.01460).
- "Reweighting the calibration set (density ratio or proximity) restores coverage for LLM conformal methods" is claimed by DS-CP (2025) and CoFact (ICLR 2026), and in the LLM-judge setting by CS-WCP.
- "A finite-sample coverage bound under non-exchangeability" is Barber et al. 2023.
- "Conformal p-values can flag exchangeability violations in LLM uncertainty quantification" is SConU (2025); sequential exchangeability testing exists as conformal test martingales.
- Inference: any paper framed as "weighted conformal for LLMs under shift" would be read as a DS-CP/CoFact follow-up.

## Residual gap (type: new method / adequacy check / applied evaluation / theory)
Type: adequacy check plus applied evaluation (small methods component). Observed: the two closest papers each propose a fix and show it on one or two benchmarks; neither reports (a) how much coverage an abstention rule loses as a function of a measurable shift size, separately for topic shift versus difficulty shift versus model-configuration shift, (b) a head-to-head of weighting schemes (none, proximity as in DS-CP, classifier-based density ratios on frozen sentence embeddings, likelihood-ratio-regularised weights as in Joshi et al. 2502.13030) at small calibration sizes where ratio estimation error dominates, or (c) whether a cheap pre-deployment diagnostic (score-distribution distance, estimated TV via a domain classifier, conformal p-value uniformity test) predicts the realised loss. Observed: the VLM audit 2608.19376 reports that source-domain diagnostics did not predict class-conditional failure under shift, which makes (c) an open empirical question rather than a foregone conclusion. Proposal: if pursued, frame C06 as a pre-registered measurement study of abstention rules under controlled shift with the diagnostic as the main pre-registered hypothesis, not as a new weighting method.

## Novelty score (1–5; 5 = no close predecessor found at search level) and justification
Score: 2. Observed: DS-CP (2510.05566) and CoFact (ICLR 2026) already claim the method half of the question (reweighting restores coverage for LLM conformal methods under shift), and SConU plus conformal test martingales cover exchangeability testing. Inference: what remains is an adequacy check on existing fixes and a diagnostic hypothesis that could be negative; publishable as an evaluation paper but not as a method paper.
