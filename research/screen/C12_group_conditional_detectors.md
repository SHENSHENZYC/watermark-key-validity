# C12 — Group-conditional calibration of zero-shot AI-text detectors (search-level screen, 2026-10-08)

## Candidate (one sentence)
Do zero-shot detectors (Binoculars with a 1.5B base–instruct pair; Fast-DetectGPT) keep their nominal false-positive rate across writer groups and domains, and does Mondrian (group-conditional) conformal calibration restore group-wise control at acceptable power, against a single global threshold and RAID-style evaluation (failure mechanism: calibration shift across groups, Liang et al. 2023)?

## Residual gap (type: new method / adequacy check / applied evaluation / theory)
Type: applied evaluation with an adequacy-check component; the method (Mondrian conformal) is textbook. Observed: no retrieved paper combines group-conditional conformal calibration with zero-shot detectors on writer groups defined by L1, proficiency or dialect, reports per-group FPR with finite-sample guarantees, or quantifies the power cost and the calibration-sample requirement (at least 1/α human texts per group for a finite threshold at level α). Open questions: (a) whether marginal conformal (MCP-style) leaves group FPR disparities that Mondrian removes; (b) the "group unknown at test time" case (inferred versus observed group; class-conditional versus Mondrian on predicted group); (c) whether the base–instruct pair choice (1.5B) changes group disparities; (d) domain × group interaction. Proposal: if pursued, pre-register the per-group FPR audit of Binoculars and Fast-DetectGPT on ICNALE (CEFR band × country) and RAID domains as the primary outcome, with Mondrian versus marginal conformal versus global threshold as the comparison, and report power at matched per-group FPR. Search-level statement: queries "conformal" × AI-generated/machine-generated text (1 hit, MCP), "group-conditional"/Mondrian × conformal × detection (no text-detector hits), conformal × detector × FPR × text × LLM (1 hit), and the two detector-fairness WebSearches returned no such combination. Absence is not proof of novelty.
